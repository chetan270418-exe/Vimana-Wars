# Vimana Wars PV Storyboard

## Creative brief
A single 15-second anime game PV for the existing Vimana Wars arcade shooter. The redesign is presented as an in-world celestial war console: mythic spacecraft, Asura armadas, sacred geometry, crisp telemetry, and energetic combat feedback. No real-life characters and no PvP framing.

## Visual authority
- Style source: existing Vimana Wars game identity and supplied in-game visual language.
- Style authority: `hero_vimana_wars.png` plus the existing ship, enemy, boss, and UI direction.
- Visual policy: faithful inheritance with presentation upgrade.
- Palette authority: obsidian interface surfaces, luminous cyan operational telemetry, restrained celestial gold for selection and reward states, Astra red for boss danger.
- Reference processing: preserve the identity and silhouettes of the existing game assets; do not inherit unrelated carrier backgrounds or UI clutter from source images.

## Material contributions
- `hero_vimana_wars.png`: subject + world; celestial spacecraft, cosmic-mystical battlefield, diagonal scale and depth.
- `pushpaka.png`: subject; playable ship silhouette and top-down fighter identity.
- `boss_ravana.png`: subject; armored boss silhouette and mono-eye threat cue.
- `vimana_wars_logo.png`: layout; title lock-up.
- Existing game UI implementation: layout + motion; chamfered glass panels, telemetry, segmented bars, pulses, scanlines, and transition overlays.

## Audio plan
Use native game-PV sound: celestial impact pulses, engine thrust, weapon fire, warning siren, boss roar, and a rising combat bed. No subtitles.

## Panels

### Panel 1 | 0.0–2.5 s
Framing: wide cosmic battlefield. The Vimana Wars logo resolves over drifting sacred geometry and layered spacecraft silhouettes. Camera: slow forward drift with a diagonal energy trail. Sound: low engine swell and UI boot pulse. Continuity: establishes the celestial war console.

### Panel 2 | 2.5–5.0 s
Framing: close top-down Pushpaka reveal inside a redesigned Astra Arsenal interface. The ship card illuminates, stat bars animate, and a deployment indicator locks. Camera: short push-in with a precise selection pulse. Sound: crisp selection click and charged hum. Continuity: the chosen ship becomes the active combat subject.

### Panel 3 | 5.0–8.5 s
Framing: Mission Control HUD over live combat. Pushpaka banks through Asura chasers and ranged vessels; projectiles streak past cockpit telemetry, combo and Astra meters climb, and a dodge leaves a clean energy wake. Camera: rapid lateral tracking with one controlled impact shake. Sound: weapon fire, dash chime, impact sparks. Continuity: energy and combo momentum build toward the boss.

### Panel 4 | 8.5–11.5 s
Framing: the HUD compresses into a boss warning lock-up as Ravana rises from the cosmic void, centered and imposing behind warning brackets. Camera: snap zoom out from the player ship to reveal scale. Sound: warning siren, bass drop, boss roar. Continuity: danger state overtakes the combat UI.

### Panel 5 | 11.5–13.5 s
Framing: Pushpaka unleashes an Astra overdrive burst through the boss arena; geometric energy layers and impact fragments fill the frame without obscuring the ship silhouette. Camera: forward charge, brief hit-stop, then a clean recoil. Sound: escalating beam tone and explosive hit.

### Panel 6 | 13.5–15.0 s
Framing: victory lock-up. Ravana breaks into controlled particles; the redesigned HUD settles into a clean VICTORY state with reward telemetry and the Vimana Wars logo returning. Camera: stable hold after the impact. Sound: wave-clear chime and resolved final tone.

## Logical shots

### Logical Shot 1 | 0.0–2.5 s | Establish the war console
Start on cosmic void and layered ships. Drift toward the diagonal battlefield and resolve the Vimana Wars logo with sacred-geometry telemetry. End on a bright transition line that slices into the arsenal.

### Logical Shot 2 | 2.5–5.0 s | Establish the chosen vessel
Start with Pushpaka centered in Astra Arsenal. Animate card selection, segmented stats, and deployment lock. End when the selected frame pulses cyan and gold, carrying the transition into combat.

### Logical Shot 3 | 5.0–8.5 s | Prove combat readability and motion
Start with Pushpaka entering the HUD. Track its bank, firing, dash, and Astra meter rise against readable Asura threats. End on a warning bracket that catches the edge of a larger silhouette.

### Logical Shot 4 | 8.5–11.5 s | Reveal the boss threat
Start from the bracketed silhouette and pull back to reveal Ravana. Compress the cockpit telemetry, trigger the boss alert, and hold the mono-eye threat cue. End on Pushpaka pointed toward the center.

### Logical Shot 5 | 11.5–13.5 s | Deliver the Astra payoff
Start with the player ship charging. Surge forward through layered geometric energy, land one decisive impact, and use controlled hit-stop before the recoil. End with the boss silhouette fragmenting.

### Logical Shot 6 | 13.5–15.0 s | Resolve with a stable reward state
Start on dissipating fragments and settle the interface into a clear VICTORY lock-up. Return the logo and hold long enough to read the finished product identity.

## Master timeline
- 0.0–2.5 s: cosmic establish → logo resolve → arsenal transition.
- 2.5–5.0 s: Pushpaka selection → stats animate → deployment lock.
- 5.0–8.5 s: combat HUD → firing, dodge, Astra charge → threat bracket.
- 8.5–11.5 s: Ravana reveal → boss warning state → attack alignment.
- 11.5–13.5 s: Astra overdrive → decisive impact → controlled recoil.
- 13.5–15.0 s: fragments clear → VICTORY lock-up → logo hold.

## Final state
One coherent game PV that sells the improved UI, ship identity, combat feedback, boss escalation, and victory payoff as a single Vimana Wars product experience.
