## Art
- Selected ship and boss silhouettes in `assets/images/` are from Kenney's
  [Space Shooter Extension](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-shooter-extension),
  licensed [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Audio
- The short WAV gameplay effects in assets/sounds/ are generated locally by
  game/systems/audio_synthesizer.py.
- combat_loop.mp3 — “8-bit Epic Space Shooter Music” by HydroGene,
  [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), downloaded from
  [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-epic-space-shooter-music).
- Selected laser, impact, explosion, and thruster effects in `assets/sounds/`
  are from Kenney's [Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds),
  licensed [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Generated
  WAV effects remain fallback variants.
